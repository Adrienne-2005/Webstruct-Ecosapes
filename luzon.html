    • Luzon

    Luzon is a tapestry of contrasts, where misty mountains, terraced rice fields, and active volcanoes like Mayon rise dramatically against the sky. Its coastlines offer hidden coves and white sand beaches in Ilocos and Batangas, with waters that shift from deep blue to turquoise. Beyond the scenery, the island’s beauty is alive in the warmth of its people and the color of its festivals that make every place feel unforgettable.
