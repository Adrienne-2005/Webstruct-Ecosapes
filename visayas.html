    • Visayas

    The Visayas captivates with its crystal-clear seas and powdery white beaches, from the famed shores of Boracay to the secluded sandbars of Kalanggaman Island. Inland, rolling hills, lush rice terraces, and waterfalls like Kawasan Falls reveal a landscape shaped by both calm and raw natural power. The region’s beauty is completed by its vibrant culture, where historic churches, colorful festivals, and the warmth of the Visayan people make every island feel alive and welcoming.
