    THREE MAIN REGIONS

    These are the Philippines' three main regions. Each has a distinctive biodiversity, an incredible geography, and stunning landscapes. Explore different locations from three main group of islands.

    Luzon

    The largest and most important island of the Philippines. Located on the northern part of the Philippine archipelago, it is bounded by the Philippine Sea, Sibuyan Sea, and the West Philippine Sea.

    Visayas

    Consists of seven large and several hundred smaller islands clustered around the Visayan, Samar, and Camotes seas. The seven main islands are Bohol, Cebu, Leyte, Masbate, Negros, Panay, and Samar.

    Mindanao

    The second largest in the Philippines, in the southern part of the archipelago, surrounded by the Bohol, Celebes, and Sulu seas. It also has the largest concentration of ethnic minorities in the Philippines.

    FACTS ABOUT PHILIPPINES

    Philippines also known as "the pearl of orient seas" is an archipelagic country located in Southeast Asia with a total area of about 300,000 square kilometers with consist of 7,641 islands and have a three main island group - which is Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao. With a population of over 114 million, it is the world's twelfth-most-populous country.
